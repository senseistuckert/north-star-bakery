Our Story

Founded in 2018, North Star Bakery began as a small home kitchen operation with a simple goal: bringing wholesome, handmade bread to our local neighborhood.

Behind the Scenes

Sourcing Philosophy

We partner directly with regional grain farmers and local dairy producers. We believe in using 100% organic, non-GMO flour without additives or artificial preservatives.
